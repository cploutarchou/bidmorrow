# BidMorrow website design audit (2026-09-01)

Scope: every public-facing route of `apps/web` (marketing pages, auth
entry pages, the shared header/footer/consent chrome). Evidence:
`scripts/design-screenshots.mjs` captures at 390/768/1440 px in both
themes (`artifacts/design-review/before/`, local, git-ignored), Lighthouse
12.8 against the local stack (`artifacts/design-review/lighthouse/before/`),
and a file-by-file inventory of the JSX, CSS and SVG components. The plan
that acts on it is `docs/design-redesign-plan.md`; what shipped is in
`docs/design-upgrade-report.md`.

Severity: **P0** damages trust or is broken · **P1** visibly weak ·
**P2** meaningful improvement · **P3** polish.

## What is already good (keep)

- Typography and hierarchy: Archivo display at real weight, mono for
  figures, a clear eyebrow / h1 / standfirst rhythm on every page.
- The palette: one teal accent on neutral surfaces, muted signal colours,
  a first-class light and dark theme, WCAG-tuned inks (`--ink-3`,
  `--sig-caution` already corrected for AA).
- Honest copy everywhere: illustrative data is labelled, buyers are
  anonymised, the decision-support disclaimer and TED attribution sit in
  the footer, the comparison table names its basis.
- The demo feed panel: a real product-shaped simulation (score ring,
  fit chip, deadline pill, component bars, risk strip) with a country
  picker. This is the strongest visual on the site today.
- Sample verdicts and the cybersecurity page: real engine output with
  breakdown tables and links to the original notices.
- The faint technical grid and quiet accent dots behind the hero.
- Motion discipline: everything sits behind `prefers-reduced-motion`
  (a global kill rule plus per-block guards), scroll reveal is progressive
  (content visible without JS), the headline word-stagger runs once.
- Accessibility baseline: Lighthouse a11y 100 on Home, axe suite green,
  skip link, labelled controls, keyboard-safe carousel and menu.
- No stock photography, no third-party embeds, no CDN assets; seven
  raster files in total, all brand/OG.

## Page transitions and empty states

- Route change uses the View Transitions cross-fade where supported; no
  change needed.
- The public site has no empty states (all content is static); the app's
  empty-state illustrations are out of scope and stay.

## Responsive notes (390 / 768 / 1440 captures)

- No horizontal overflow on any route at any width (script check).
- Hero at 390 px: copy first, art below the fold; with the consent banner
  open the CTA note is hidden (finding #4). The new hero composition for
  390 px is specified in the plan (≈ 420 px tall, four signal rows, 2×2
  bands).
- 768 px: single-column hero with the art centred; fine.
- Full-page captures of below-the-fold reveal sections need the observer
  to fire; the capture script now paces its scroll for that. Not a site
  defect (content is visible without JS and under reduced motion).

## Not changing (deliberately)

URLs, `PageMeta`/`MARKETING_META` titles and descriptions, canonical and
OG tags, the sitemap, forms and auth flows, the Paddle checkout entry,
analytics/consent behaviour, API calls, the comparison table's substance,
pricing copy, disclaimers and attribution.
