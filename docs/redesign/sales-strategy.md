## 0. The honest-selling rule (standing, applies to every asset below)

Non-negotiable, and it is also our sharpest differentiator, so we lean in:

- **Never promise outcomes.** No "win more bids," no "winnable tenders,"
  no eligibility/award language. We sell **decision quality** and **time
  saved on the no's** — a faster, defensible bid/no-bid call.
- **The disclaimer stays intact and visible.** BidMorrow is decision
  support; customers verify requirements in the original procurement
  documents. Risk flags say "Possible requirement detected — verify in
  source documents," never "you are eligible/ineligible."
- **No fake proof.** Zero customers today → founding-cap honesty ("first
  20"), never a logo wall, never a fabricated stat. TED statistics we
  quote are about TED (true, sourced), never dressed up as product
  metrics.
- **Coverage honesty first.** We state what we do NOT cover before a
  prospect finds it. Honesty stated first beats honesty extracted later —
  and it is a trust asset the free tiers cannot cheaply copy.

## 1. Positioning

### One-line positioning

**The only bid/no-bid tool whose verdict is auditable arithmetic you can
re-run — the explanation and the reasons to walk away included on every
tender, flat €29, no meters — not an AI rationale you have to trust.**

### Positioning statement

For 5–50-person EU IT, cyber and software consultancies that pursue
public-sector work without a dedicated bid team, **BidMorrow** is
**deterministic bid/no-bid qualification on official TED data**. It scores
every in-scope competition notice 0–100 and shows the arithmetic behind
the verdict: **the same tender always gets the same score, every point is
traceable to a rule you can read, and the explanation — plus the hard
exclusions and risk flags that tell you when to walk away — is included on
every scored tender, never metered, never a paid upgrade.** Unlike the
AI-matching tools now crowding this category, BidMorrow's verdict is
**reproducible and auditable, not generated prose**, and it is flat
€29/€49 EUR with no credits, no reveals, no workspace quotas.

### Differentiation narrative — the key insight

The assessment's central finding is the spine of our story: **the category
meters the score and paywalls the explanation.** GetTenderAI (VERIFIED)
gives free users 5 match-score checks a month, charges €235 for unlimited
scoring, and paywalls the _relevance explanation_ one tier higher at €319.
Across the field, the "reason a tender fits" is **LLM-generated prose** —
non-reproducible, un-auditable, and often sold as the premium layer.

BidMorrow inverts every part of that:

- **They meter the score. We include it, unlimited.** Every in-scope
  notice, scored, always.
- **They paywall the explanation. We include it, decomposed.** The
  components sum to the total; every point cites the rule and evidence
  that produced it.
- **They generate a rationale you must trust. We compute arithmetic you
  can re-run.** No LLM in the scoring path means _same inputs + same
  engine version ⇒ identical score and components_ — a verifiable
  invariant, not a marketing adjective.

**Escalate the claim.** In 2026, "a match score with a written reason" is
table stakes — Tendly, Stotles, TenderStria and others all say it. So we
stop competing on _that we explain_ and move up one level to _how our
explanation is built_: **auditable arithmetic you can re-run, not
generated prose.** That is proof, not louder copy — and proof is the only
thing that survives claim-commoditization (R2).

### What ONLY BidMorrow can truthfully say (the defensible set)

Each of these is checkable and, per the assessment, uncontested by any
entrant found:

1. **"Same tender in, same score out — every time — because there is no
   LLM in the scoring path."** (Determinism/reproducibility. Matching-
   engine invariant #1.) No entrant can say this truthfully; every rival
   "reason" found is LLM-generated.
2. **"Every point in the verdict is traceable to a rule you can read, and
   the components sum to the total."** (Decomposable/auditable arithmetic.
   Invariant #2.)
3. **"The explanation is included on every scored tender — never metered,
   never a paid upgrade."** (Aimed squarely at the observed category
   pattern; verifiable against our own flat pricing.)
4. **"We also score when to walk away."** Hard exclusions and risk flags
   with the evidence quoted from the source. Competitors score _fit_; none
   observed scores _disqualification_. This is the half of the verdict a
   capacity-constrained firm actually pays for.
5. **"Official TED provenance, with attribution, and a coverage statement
   that says plainly what we do and don't cover."** Nobody in the field
   leads with data provenance; coverage claims are portal-count bragging.
6. **"Built for one buyer."** 5–50-person EU IT/cyber/software
   consultancies without a bid team — depth a sector-agnostic generalist
   is structurally unable to match.

**Demoted to table stakes** (do not lead with these): a score-with-an-
explanation _as a claim_; EU-native EUR pricing; the €29 number itself;
friction-free discovery. The distinctive remainder of "€29" is **flat with
no meters** — no credits, reveals, or workspace quotas — which no entrant
offers. Lead with the shape, not the number.

### The frame that keeps us out of everyone's lane: push, not pull

Search tools (GetTenderAI most sharply) answer **"find me tenders about
X."** BidMorrow answers **"of everything published, which merit your next
two weeks, and why."** They _pull_ candidates on demand; we _push_ a
standing verdict against a standing profile. Keep this pull-vs-push
distinction visible — it is why a free semantic-search tier is not a
substitute for what we do, and it is the articulation that replaces the
now-occupied reduction narrative (see §4, R9).

## 3. Objection handling (ICP: 5–50-person EU IT/cyber consultancy)

Truthful answers only. No disparagement of unverified specifics.

### 3.1 "Why not GetTenderAI's free tier?"

Their free tier is 5 match-score checks a month and semantic search over a
mostly-French catalog; unlimited scoring is €235 and the explanation of
_why_ is €319. If you bid a handful of times a year and want to actually
understand each verdict, five metered checks with no explanation is a taste
designed to hit a wall. BidMorrow scores every in-scope tender, unlimited,
with the full decomposed explanation and walk-away flags, at €29 — there
is no meter to hit. And it's a different job: they answer "search for X,"
we answer "of everything published, which merit your time, and why."
_Honest caveat:_ if you win mostly French **sub-threshold** contracts,
their BOAMP coverage sees notices we don't — we're official-TED, above
threshold, EU-wide.

### 3.2 "Why not the free tier of [Tendly / one of the AI matchers]?" _(PROVISIONAL — no price quoting)_

Several tools now offer a free, credit-metered tier with an AI "reason it
fits." Two honest distinctions hold regardless of their exact price: (a) a
credit-metered free tier is a taste that runs out; our paid entry is
**unmetered** — every tender scored and explained, always; (b) their
"reason" is **generated prose you have to trust**, while ours is
**arithmetic you can re-run and audit** — plus we show the walk-away half
they don't. We don't quote their price and we don't claim they lack a free
tier; we compete on the shape of the answer.

### 3.3 "Why pay €29 when X gives me a free scan?"

A free scan (Stotles' report, or free credits elsewhere) is a **one-off
generated output** serving an upsell or a metered taste. You can't re-run
it and get the same answer, you can't see the arithmetic behind it, and it
doesn't tell you when to **walk away**. €29 flat buys continuous,
unlimited, **reproducible** verdicts with evidence and hard exclusions on
every matching TED notice — no credit pack, no reveal quota, no workspace
cap. You're paying for decision quality and the time you save on the no's,
not a teaser. (We don't promise you'll win — we promise a defensible call,
faster.)

### 3.4 "Why not the AI one?"

Fair question — and here's the precise answer, not an anti-AI slogan. The
AI tools generate a written rationale per tender. It reads well, but it's
**non-deterministic** (the same tender can be explained differently on
different runs) and you can't audit the number behind it. BidMorrow is
deliberately **LLM-free in the scoring path**: same tender in, same score
out, every point traceable to a rule you can read. For a security/IT
consultancy that has to defend "why did we pursue this?" to a managing
partner, **auditable arithmetic beats persuasive prose.** (We never call
ourselves "AI-powered" — we state, truthfully, that the engine is
deterministic and LLM-free; and we don't call their AI "wrong," we call
ours reproducible.)

### 3.5 "Your coverage is narrower."

True — and we say so up front. We cover official **TED** competition
notices, EU-wide, within a documented CPV scope (72*/48*/79417000),
**above threshold**. We don't ingest national sub-threshold bulletins, so
a France-focused rival that reads BOAMP will see some French below-
threshold contracts we don't. We'd rather tell you now than have you find
it later. In exchange you get one **official source with mandatory
attribution**, visible freshness, an honest CPV scope, and a verdict whose
provenance you can trust — not a "2,000+ portals" number that hides DE 12 ·
IT 2 · ES 0. If your pipeline is mostly national sub-threshold, we're
honestly not your best tool yet. _(That's a qualify-out, not a pitch —
and stating it is itself the trust posture that wins the buyers we're
right for.)_

### 3.6 "You're far cheaper than the named tools and below several free tiers — is this a real product? Will you be around?"

_(The viability objection the assessment under-weights — handle it head
on.)_ The price is flat because the economics are structurally cheap, not
because it's a fire-sale: the engine is deterministic and LLM-free, so
there's no per-query model cost, and TED is a single official source. We're
honest about being early — a solo builder, a "first 20" founding cap
that's wired to the real number, price on the page, no lock-in, cancel
anytime. We sell decision quality, not a discount, and we don't over-
promise longevity — we remove the risk of committing to us (monthly, no
lock-in) instead.

## 6. Where I judge the assessment WEAK or overstated (challenge in writing)

The assessment is strong, well-evidenced, and appropriately hedged on
snippet-grade items. Six specific challenges:

1. **The "pricing gift / 8–14x validation" narrative is comforting but
   overstated as near-certain.** GetTenderAI's €235 bundles semantic
   search + document analysis + unlimited scores over a broad, France-
   heavy catalog. That is _not_ clean proof that a tiny IT/cyber
   consultancy will pay hundreds for **scoring**, nor that our €29 auto-
   reads as "honest, not budget." Anchoring from _far_ above can equally
   read as "these are different products." The assessment itself concedes
   (R4) the anchor only helps if methodology carries the credibility.
   **Recommendation:** use the anchor sparingly, always paired with the
   proof; never let "look how cheap we are vs €235" become the pitch — it
   invites the toy/viability doubt.

2. **The viability/longevity objection is under-weighted.** A price ~8x
   below the nearest named tool _and_ below several free tiers, from a
   sole trader with a "first 20" cap, predictably triggers "is this real /
   will it last?" The assessment flags the "toy" risk but not the
   longevity anxiety. Handled as objection §3.6 (structural-cost
   explanation + no lock-in + honest earliness). This should be a standard
   part of the pitch, not an afterthought.

3. **R9 (reduction-narrative collision) is rated Medium/stable, but its
   practical bite is larger than "Medium" implies** — it lands on the
   single most valuable asset (the hero) _and_ the Strata concept is
   literally a reduction funnel (Discover → Qualify → Decide). Treat it as
   a **hard copy constraint on the hero**, not a background risk (see L5).

4. **R10 (sub-threshold gap) is correctly Medium at portfolio level, but
   for a French-sub-threshold-dependent prospect it is a genuine
   qualify-out, not an objection to overcome.** Sales should disqualify
   that sub-segment cleanly rather than fight a coverage battle we lose on

5. **"No LLM competitor can publish a reproducible methodology; make them

   **determinism itself**, which they cannot truthfully claim. Don't let
   "we published a page" become the pitch — the page is the vehicle,
   reproducibility is the moat.

6. **Compliance note (not a disagreement): honor the PROVISIONAL labels.**
   Tendly's price (€ vs $) and Tenderium's tiers/PAYG are snippet-grade;
   the strategy above never asserts them as fact and never hinges on them.
