# Approval-checkpoint package (Stage 3)

Present ALL items, then STOP for owner selection/approval:

1. Codebase and product findings (what exists, constraints, UX defects).
2. Competitor findings with source links and screenshots.
3. UX, conversion, and SEO opportunities (prioritized).
4. Proposed sitemap and user journeys.
5. Keyword-to-page strategy (search intent per public page).
6. At least three substantially different design directions (checked
   against the rejected-directions registry in requirements.md).
7. Desktop and mobile mockups for each direction, both themes.
8. Proposed images, visual assets, motion, and interactions (with origin
   and licensing for every asset class).
9. Design-system foundations (tokens, type, spacing, components).
10. Multilingual architecture (library choice, catalog design, activation
    process).
11. Recommended libraries with benefits and tradeoffs (per the vetting
    rules in requirements.md §Libraries).
12. The strongest recommended direction and the reasoning.
13. Implementation milestones, dependencies, risks, and measurable
    acceptance criteria.

Rules: no major production-code changes before approval; record the
owner's decision in requirements.md §Decisions log and the ledger.
