# Design direction — <name>

- **Concept thesis**: one paragraph — the idea, not the styling. What does
  this direction believe about how the product should be presented?
- **How it differs** from the other candidate directions AND from every
  rejected direction in requirements.md (substance, not palette).
- **Mockups**: desktop + mobile, both themes — links/paths.

## Identity

- Type system: faces (self-hosted, licensed), scale, roles.
- Color tokens: light + dark sets; accent logic; semantic colors.
- Space/radius/shadow/depth system; translucency policy + fallback.
- Iconography and illustration/imagery language.

## Layout model

Grid, page rhythm, hero pattern, section anatomy, responsive behavior.

## Motion and micro-interactions

What moves, why (explain/guide/feedback), durations/easings,
reduced-motion behavior.

## Product storytelling

How the product is demonstrated (demo, screenshots, narrative devices);
onboarding treatment.

## Asset manifest

Every required asset: purpose, format, dimensions, responsive variants,
alt text, loading behavior, origin (original/generated/licensed).

## Feasibility

CSP compliance, bundle/perf cost, WCAG 2.2 AA risks and mitigations,
implementation effort estimate in the existing stack.
